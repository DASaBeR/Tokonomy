## Overall task
Sale prices are rounded per unit, which makes multi-quantity lines wrong by a cent. The rounding lives in the
helper shop.money.round_line(price, qty); fix it there. In shop/cart.py a line on sale must be computed as
round2(unit_price * (100 - SALE_PERCENT) / 100 * qty): rounded once on the line total, not per unit. Example:
unit price 0.99, qty 3, on sale (20% off) is 2.38, not 2.37. Lines not on sale are plain round2(unit_price * qty).
Keep shop.pricing.sale_price(unit_price) unchanged for single units. Add unit tests.

## Architecture (decided; follow it)
Change Cart.line_total so that, when the cart is on sale, it applies the sale percent to unit_price * qty and calls round2 once. Non-sale lines stay round2(unit_price * qty). sale_price in pricing.py is untouched and remains the single-unit API. Add tests to tests/test_cart.py and tests/test_pricing.py.

Components:
- Cart.line_total (modify shop/cart.py): Compute the line total, rounding once per line.
- cart tests (modify tests/test_cart.py): Cover multi-quantity sale rounding and non-sale lines.
- pricing tests (modify tests/test_pricing.py): Pin the unchanged single-unit sale_price behaviour.

## Decisions that bind this unit
- ADR-1 Fix in Cart.line_total, not a non-existent round_line: Implement the formula inline in Cart.line_total, importing SALE_PERCENT from shop.pricing. Do not create shop.money.round_line.
  Why: round_line does not exist. The task explicitly gives the formula for shop/cart.py, and a minimal change avoids new public API.
  Rejected: Creating round_line in money.py: it would invent an API the task assumed already existed, and money.py would need to know about the sale percent.; Changing sale_price: this is forbidden by the task.
  Constraint: Do not add functions to shop/money.py.
  Constraint: Do not modify shop/pricing.py.
- ADR-2 Exact expression order: Use round2(item["unit_price"] * (100 - SALE_PERCENT) / 100 * item["qty"]) in exactly this left-to-right order.
  Why: It matches the spec and keeps float results predictable.
  Rejected: Multiplying by 0.8 or reordering the operations.
  Constraint: Do not hard-code 20 or 0.8.
