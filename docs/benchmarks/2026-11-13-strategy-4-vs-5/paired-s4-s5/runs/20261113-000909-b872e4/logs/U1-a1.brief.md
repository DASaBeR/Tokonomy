## Decisions that bind this unit
- ADR-1 to_cents rounding implementation: Implement to_cents as int((Decimal(repr(amount)) * 100).quantize(Decimal("1"), rounding=ROUND_HALF_UP)), importing Decimal and ROUND_HALF_UP from decimal.
  Why: repr gives the shortest decimal string that round-trips, so 19.99 becomes exactly 1999, 1.005 becomes 101 and 0.30000000000000004 becomes 30. The rounding is exact half up.
  Rejected: int(amount * 100 + 0.5): fails for 1.005 and 0.285; round(amount * 100): uses banker's rounding; Decimal(amount) without repr: exposes the binary error, so 1.005 becomes 100
  Constraint: Do not use round() or a float epsilon in to_cents.
- ADR-2 Helpers live only in shop/money.py: Create to_cents/from_cents directly in shop/money.py. Do not create shop/currency.py.
  Why: shop/currency.py does not exist in the repository.
  Rejected: Creating a shop/currency.py shim
  Constraint: No file named shop/currency.py may exist.
