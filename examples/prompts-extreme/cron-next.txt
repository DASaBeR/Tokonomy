Add shop/cron.py with next_run(expr: str, after: datetime.datetime) -> datetime.datetime for a 5-field cron expression: minute 0-59, hour 0-23, day-of-month 1-31, month 1-12, day-of-week 0-7, separated by runs of whitespace (leading/trailing whitespace ignored). A field is a comma list of terms: N, A-B or *, where A-B and * may carry a /S step (S >= 1) and a bare A may too. Numbers are ASCII digits only (leading zeros fine, no sign). "*" is the full range (day-of-week 0-6); "A/S" means A through the field maximum (7 for day-of-week), so "5/15" in minutes is 5,20,35,50; "A-B/S" steps from A to B. A > B is invalid. Month accepts jan..dec and day-of-week sun..sat (sun=0, sat=6), case-insensitive, wherever a number is allowed in that field and nowhere else; day-of-week 7 is also Sunday. Naive datetimes are UTC. Seconds and microseconds of `after` are ignored; return the earliest matching minute strictly later than after's minute, naive, with second=0. A day matches if its month matches and: when both day-of-month and day-of-week are restricted, either one matching is enough; when only one is restricted, only it is checked; when neither is, every day matches. A field is unrestricted only if its text is exactly "*", so "*/2" and "*/1" are restricted. Only years up to after.year + 8 are searched; if nothing matches (e.g. "0 0 30 2 *") raise ValueError. ValueError is also raised for any malformed expression (field count, out-of-range value, step 0, empty list item or step, unknown name, name in the wrong field) and for a timezone-aware `after`. Add unit tests.
